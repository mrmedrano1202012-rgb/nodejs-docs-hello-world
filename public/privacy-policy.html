Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Super Cleaners Detailing ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile auto detailing business serving Bakersfield, California and the surrounding Kern County area. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, submitting a booking request, or communicating with us, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information: your name, mobile phone number, and email address (email is optional).
  • Service information: the address where service will be performed, ZIP code, vehicle year/make/model and size, the service package you select, and any notes you provide (for example, gate codes or vehicle condition).
  • Booking and payment information: appointment date and time, price estimate, how you choose to pay, and — if you pay by card and choose or are required to save a card on file — a payment token processed and stored by our payment processor (Square). We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Messaging consent records: whether you opted in to booking text messages and/or offers-and-reminders text messages, and when that consent was given (online checkbox or verbal consent on a phone booking).

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your booking request, schedule and perform your detail, and communicate with you about your appointment.
  • To send the text messages described in Section 3, only if you have opted in.
  • To process payment for services.
  • To maintain our business records, including consent records we are required to keep for our messaging program.

3. Text messaging (SMS) privacy

If you opt in, we use your mobile number to send booking confirmations, appointment reminders, and scheduling updates. If you separately opt in to offers and reminders, we may also send review requests, rebooking reminders, and occasional offers (maximum about 4 messages per month). Message frequency varies by booking activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your phone number or any other personal information. We do not use purchased or rented contact lists.

4. When we share information

Other than the mobile-information commitment above, we share information only as needed to run the business and only with service providers that help us operate — for example, our payment processor (Square) to process card payments and store cards on file, and our messaging provider (Twilio) to deliver the text messages you opted in to. These providers may use the information only to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep booking and consent records for as long as needed to provide service, maintain accurate business and tax records, and demonstrate messaging consent — generally up to four years after your last booking — and then delete or anonymize them. If you ask us to delete your information sooner, we will do so except where we must keep a record by law or to honor an opt-out (for example, keeping your number on our do-not-text list so we do not message you again).

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, including access controls on our booking system and encrypted connections (HTTPS) for our online form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information against unauthorized access.

7. Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP at any time to stop all text messages. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (661) 376-5857.
  • Access and deletion: contact us at (661) 376-5857 to ask what information we hold about you or to request deletion, subject to Section 5.
  • Accuracy: contact us to correct information that is wrong.

8. Children

Our services are intended for adults scheduling vehicle detailing. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this URL and update the "Last updated" date above.
