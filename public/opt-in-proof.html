How Customers Opt In to Text Messages

SMS opt-in documentation for Super Cleaners Detailing, a mobile auto detailing business in Bakersfield, CA. Business phone: (661) 376-5857. Text messages are sent from (661) 387-3892. Customers opt in two ways: (1) online, through the booking request form, or (2) verbally, when booking by phone. Both methods are documented below.

1. Online opt-in — the booking request form

Customers who book online use the booking request form hosted at mistyrose-echidna-440550.hostingersite.com (linked from supercleanersdetailing.com). In Step 5 of the form ("Payment & send request"), under the heading "Text message consent," the customer sees two checkboxes. Both checkboxes are unchecked by default — consent is never pre-checked, and each type of message requires its own separate, explicit opt-in. The exact text shown to the customer is:

Text message consent
Booking texts. Text me about my booking — confirmations, reminders, and scheduling updates from Super Cleaners Detailing. Frequency varies by booking activity.
Offers & reminders (optional). Text me review requests, rebooking reminders, and occasional offers. Max ~4 messages/month.

Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

The customer must also check a separate box agreeing to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before the form can be submitted. A customer who does not check "Booking texts" receives no text messages about their booking.

2. How consent is captured and logged

When the form is submitted, the customer's consent choices are stored in the booking record in our booking database, together with the booking itself. Each record keeps:

  • Customer name and mobile phone number
  • Whether transactional ("Booking texts") consent was given — yes/no
  • Whether marketing ("Offers & reminders") consent was given — yes/no
  • Confirmation that the customer agreed to the Privacy Policy and Terms
  • Date and time the request was submitted

3. First message the customer receives

After John confirms the booking personally, the customer receives a confirmation text from (661) 387-3892. Sample confirmation message:

Super Cleaners Detailing: Hi Maria, your detail is confirmed for Fri Oct 23 at 4:30 PM. We'll text a reminder before your visit. Reply STOP to end, HELP for help.

Every message identifies Super Cleaners Detailing as the sender. Replying STOP ends all messages at any time; replying HELP reaches us. Message frequency varies by booking activity; message and data rates may apply.

4. Verbal opt-in — customers who book by phone

Some customers book by calling (661) 376-5857 instead of using the online form. On every phone booking, before any text is sent, staff read this script and the customer must say yes:

"Before we finish — we send appointment texts: a confirmation today, a reminder before your visit, and a text if anything changes. Is it okay to text those to this number? Message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime to end them. Our privacy policy is on our website at supercleanersdetailing.com/privacy-policy."
  • The customer's yes/no answer is recorded in the booking record, marked as verbal consent, along with the date, time, and the name of the staff member who read the script.
  • If the customer says no, the booking is completed without texting and the record is marked "SMS consent: declined."
  • The first text a phone customer receives is the same confirmation message shown in Section 3.
